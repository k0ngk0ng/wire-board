- Installing C4 replaces the ordinary round timer with a visible explosion deadline on PC or touch screens. The planted state must hide that timer without removing planting/defusing progress.
- The touch round timer remains beneath toolbar, HUD or inventory rows instead of the top safe edge of the actual game area.
- Global C4 audio requires hidden bomb coordinates, a private explosion deadline, another player's defuser identity or a defuse deadline.
- The current cue starts on first connection, recovery, unmute, foreground return or a multi-cue network gap. Repeated projections or stage changes within the same serial restart the same sound.
- Muting, pausing, backgrounding, a new epoch/round, defuse or explosion leaves a queued/active C4 sound that resumes later.
- Five original C4 samples are replaced with generic beeps, use spatial attenuation despite the user's global-audio preference, or steal the gunfire/announcement channel.
- The first beep, periodic sequence, integer packing or wave stages diverge from the agreed 100-FPS audio projection contract.
- 320px, Duo and Pad ordinary/native-fullscreen/page-fullscreen layouts clip or cover the clock. Source-audio identity checks are mistaken for original-game runtime or physical-device certification.
