# Impact effect failure inventory

- Unarmored bullet and knife hits have no authoritative blood effect.
- Protected torso hits bleed, or helmet hits bleed instead of sparking.
- Generic and leg hits incorrectly inherit torso armor suppression.
- The hit which exhausts armor uses the post-damage state instead of the pre-hit type.
- A legacy zero-value helmet is mistaken for no armor type.
- HE, C4 or falling damage produces bullet helmet sparks.
- Friendly-fire rejection, dead targets, persistence or projection invents effects or discloses armor.
- Missing effects enlarge every ordinary event instead of remaining omitted.
